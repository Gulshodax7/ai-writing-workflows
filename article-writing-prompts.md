# AI Article Writing & Productivity Workflows

A collection of structured prompts and templates for researching, outlining, and writing high-quality articles using Claude and MCP tools.

## 1. Research & Outline Prompt
"Analyze the topic [TOPIC]. Break it down into 5 key subtopics, identify common misconceptions, and create a logical outline with compelling hooks for each section."

## 2. Fact-Checking & Depth Prompt
"Review the draft below. Highlight any vague claims that need data or real-world examples, and suggest concrete improvements to make the argument sharper."

## 3. Style & Readability Editor
"Edit this article for clarity, flow, and active voice. Eliminate fluff, shorten overly complex sentences, and ensure smooth transitions between paragraphs while preserving my original tone."
